Data, maps and things I wanted to understand.

I’m George, a data scientist at Cisco in London. I work with large, messy network datasets, usually trying to work out whether a pattern is real, what caused it and how to explain it clearly.

My background is in spatial networks and human movement. Outside work, I build things around music, transport and cities because I wanted them to exist or had a question I wanted to answer.
