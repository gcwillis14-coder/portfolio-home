2022

Using the web to predict regional trade flows: data extraction, modelling, and validation

Web hyperlink data between UK regional domains is extracted, modelled and validated as a proxy signal for inter-regional trade — testing whether "who links to whom" online tracks who trades with whom in the real economy.

Tranos, E., Carrascal-Incera, A. & Willis, G. (2022). Annals of the American Association of Geographers.
2021

Using "Big Data" to understand the impacts of Uber on taxis in New York City

NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission records are combined with New York Times API data on Uber's popularity, then run through time-series models to test how Uber's growth affected New York's established yellow-taxi market.

Willis, G. & Tranos, E. (2021). Travel Behaviour and Society, 22: 94–107.

Both papers grew out of the regional hyperlinks and data-processing work in the project index.
