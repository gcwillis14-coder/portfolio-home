Publications

Peer-reviewed papers resulting from spatial econometric models and web network analysis. Both started out as data experiments that yielded surprisingly clean results.

2022

Using the web to predict regional trade flows: data extraction, modelling, and validation

Web hyperlink networks between UK regional domains are extracted, geocoded, and evaluated as a proxy for inter-regional economic transactions. The findings demonstrate that digital linkage structure tracks official inter-regional trade data with substantial predictive accuracy.

Tranos, E., Carrascal-Incera, A. & Willis, G. (2022). Annals of the American Association of Geographers, 113(1), 168-189. DOI: 10.1080/24694452.2022.2109577 →
2021

Using "Big Data" to understand the impacts of Uber on taxis in New York City

NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission trip records combined with New York Times API query data to model how ride-hailing services altered yellow taxi ridership and trip duration patterns across New York boroughs.

Willis, G. & Tranos, E. (2021). Travel Behaviour and Society, 22, 94-107. DOI: 10.1016/j.tbs.2020.08.003 →
