About

George Willis

I’m a data scientist at Cisco in London, working on internet performance. I use Python, R and SQL to understand what the measurements show and explain it clearly.

My research background is in spatial networks and human movement. I’ve worked with taxi trips, satellite imagery and links between websites to study how places change and connect.

Outside work, I build projects around things I’m interested in. GigRadar started because I kept missing gigs at small London venues. TransitFlow explores how metro networks behave when stations close.

I like understanding why something works, then building something useful with it. This site brings together those projects and my published research.
