Engineering Decisions

Custom Scraping Engine

Why

To get advanced warning of events before competitive ticket releases. Relying on DICE or Ticketmaster APIs meant finding out about gigs at the same time as the rest of the market. Scraping venue sites directly provides a "zero-day" signal.

Alternative considered

Using official ticketing APIs (DICE, Ticketmaster, Songkick).

Trade-offs

Requires ongoing maintenance of scraper abstractions due to changing venue DOM structures, but the early-warning capability makes it worthwhile.

FastAPI

Why

Required a backend that natively supports async operations for heavy concurrent polling/scraping. The tight integration with Pydantic was essential for validating structured outputs from local vision models.

Alternative considered

Django or Flask.

Trade-offs

Lacks the heavy "batteries-included" ORM/admin panel of Django, requiring manual setup of SQLAlchemy and Alembic for the data layer.

PostgreSQL + pgvector

Why

Allowed keeping relational event data and vector embeddings in the exact same ACID-compliant database. It is free and eliminates the operational complexity of managing a standalone vector database.

Alternative considered

Elasticsearch or a dedicated vector DB like Pinecone.

Trade-offs

Scaling a relational database for heavy vector search workloads is computationally demanding, but entirely acceptable for a personal-scale application.

Local AI (Ollama)

Why

Served as a sandbox for using local intelligence (restricted in corporate environments). AI is strictly bounded to semantic deduplication and "Flyer Lab" multimodal OCR—not a generic chatbot.

Alternative considered

Calling OpenAI / Anthropic APIs.

Trade-offs

Higher local compute requirements and slower inference times compared to passing payloads to cloud providers.

Cloudflare Pages (Demo Separation)

Why

Provided control and operational safety ("less spoon-fed than Vercel"). It ensures peace of mind for a hobby project—if left running indefinitely, there is no risk of massive surprise serverless bills.

Alternative considered

Vercel or AWS Amplify.

Trade-offs

Required deliberately isolating the read-only static demo from the stateful production application.

Lessons Learned

Why

If I wiped the repository today and rebuilt GigRadar tomorrow, I would plan the full stack with diagrams first and figure out the hosting and deployment strategy earlier, rather than waiting until after the initial build.

Alternative considered

Iterative, unstructured prototyping.

Trade-offs

Designing the full architecture upfront requires more initial friction but drastically reduces refactoring later.
