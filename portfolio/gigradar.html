GigRadar

A way to keep up with small London venues and find gigs before I’ve missed them.

Open demo ↗All projects →

Why I’m building it

I kept finding out about gigs after they had happened or sold out. Following individual venues meant checking several sites and hoping I hadn’t missed an announcement.

GigRadar brings that information together. The aim is simple: browse events, find venues on a map and follow the places and artists I care about.

The data work

The difficult part is keeping event information consistent across sources. Dates change, the same gig appears in more than one place, and a missing listing doesn’t necessarily mean an event is cancelled.

I’m working on source ingestion, event identity and retaining the evidence behind each update. A ticket-status label should be traceable to a source.

Demo and development

The public demo is a preview of the interface. Treat its listings as demo data, and check the venue or ticket seller for current details.

The development project uses a Python backend, SvelteKit and PostgreSQL. Source collection and data checks are developed separately from the public demo.

Flyer extraction and more detailed event signals are areas I’m exploring. They are not promises about what the demo can currently do.
